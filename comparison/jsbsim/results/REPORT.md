# Repository vs JSBSim: Cessna 172 comparison

Generated by run_comparison.py. This is a code-to-code comparison, **not independent validation against a real aircraft**.

**Condition:** 55 m/s, 1000 m, equator, heading north, still air.  
**Configuration:** c172x (symmetric payload, fixed external thrust, direct surface angles).  
**Mass / wing:** 970.7 kg / 16.17 m²; span 10.97 m.

## What is compared

The harness compares (1) rigid-body equations using identical JSBSim-applied loads, (2) trim and local modes, and (3) time responses to small elevator, aileron and rudder perturbations. The repository aerodynamic derivatives are locally fitted to JSBSim's C172 database. This helps isolate the dynamics and implementation, but it does not independently validate either aerodynamic model.

## Level 1 — equations of motion

The same JSBSim loads were evaluated at 40 randomized states. Raw maximum translational-acceleration errors were 1.267e-02, 2.662e-02 and 4.070e-02 m/s². After adding analytical rotating-Earth acceleration terms omitted by the repository, the errors were 1.39e-13, 1.77e-13 and 1.11e-12 m/s².

The remaining angular-acceleration discrepancies range from 3.40e-05 to 5.04e-05 rad/s² and have not been independently isolated. Euler-rate and NED-velocity checks are recorded in summary.json.

The phugoid is the clearest mode discrepancy: 0.2083 rad/s (JSBSim) versus 0.1788 rad/s (repository). JSBSim includes lift and pitching-moment derivatives with respect to angle-of-attack rate; the repository model does not. Disabling those two JSBSim terms diagnostically gives 0.2083 and 0.2029 rad/s. The gap narrows, but this does not prove alpha-dot is the only cause.

## Limits

- One local operating point and small control inputs; no stall, post-stall, high-angle, turning, or envelope-wide tests.
- The repository derivatives are fitted from JSBSim, not wind-tunnel or flight-test data.
- Earth rotation is corrected analytically only for the Level 1 check; the repository's active equations remain flat-Earth and non-rotating.
- The source C172 data retain their upstream license; the derived XML is generated locally and is not committed.
